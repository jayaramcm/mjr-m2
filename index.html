Done Coming up

  1. DoneStage 01 · Master plan

    The layout is approved

    The plan for 104 plots, 30 and 40 ft roads and three parks across 7.93 acres. Approval comes first: it’s what RERA registration is built on.

  2. DoneStage 02 · TG RERA P01600010465

    Registered with TG RERA

    Registration P01600010465 puts the project, its plan and its developer on record with the state regulator. Check it yourself.

  3. Coming upStage 03 · Site infrastructure

    Roads, drains and streetlights

    Blacktop 30 and 40 ft roads with kerbs, storm-water drains and streetlights, laid across the whole layout and up to every plot.

  4. Coming upStage 04 · Open spaces & parks

    Parks and amenities

    Three parks with pergolas, a pool, a lawn, play and fitness areas, a quiet park for elders and an EV charging station.

  5. Your stepStage 05 · Ready for registration

    Your plot, in your name

    Choose a plot, register it in your name and build when it suits you. The roads and services reach your boundary.
